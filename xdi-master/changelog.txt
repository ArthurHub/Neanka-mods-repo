1.3.6 VR:
- Turning the player with the thumbstick now works while the dialogue menu is open, which the game
  itself blocks. Uses the game's own VR turning settings (rotation type, angle and speed).
  MCM: VR > "Allow player to turn in dialogue" (bEnableRotation), on by default.
- The dialogue UI is kept facing the NPC being talked to, instead of drifting with head rotation and
  jumping to a new spot once the player has turned far enough. Only the direction the UI is anchored
  in changes; distance and size are left to the game. Takes effect from the next conversation.
  MCM: VR > "Keep dialogue UI towards the NPC" (bEnableDialogueUIFacingNpc), on by default.
- Exiting a conversation with the grip button can now be turned off.
  MCM: VR > "Allow exiting dialogue with grip button" (bEnableExitByGrip), on by default.
- Fixed the dialogue target reference leaking every frame, which corrupted the actor being talked to.

1.3.5 VR:
- The trigger button skips the line being spoken, by the player or by the NPC. Works at the start,
  in the middle and at the end of a conversation.
- The grip button leaves a conversation without having to walk away from the NPC. Works on dialogues
  that set their own distance too.
- Fixed a line being skipped when selecting a dialogue option - the menu sent a button-up event that
  the VR dialogue menu treats as a skip.
